The platform

What is My Talkies?

My Talkies is a dedicated digital platform built for Malayalam cinema — created to give KFCC member producers a structured, transparent way to release their films digitally, reach audiences, and earn from them on an ongoing basis.

It has been designed specifically for the needs of the Malayalam film industry, rather than as a generic aggregator platform.

Inside your dashboard

Film onboarding — your 3-step journey

Every film submitted through My Talkies goes through the same process before it moves into technical, rights and commercial review and finally goes live.

Step 01

Film information

Title, languages, genres, contributors, synopsis, CBFC details, viewer advisory and producer information.

Step 02

Assets & services

2K master, teaser, trailer, banners, poster, subtitles and promotional material, with optional DRM or B2B distribution.

Step 03

Legal documents & submit

Review your details, upload the required documents and submit the film for technical, rights and content review.

✦
Legal requirements that need your attentionThe Rights Holder Affidavit is mandatory for every film. A Producer Authorisation Letter is required whenever there is more than one Producer or Co-Producer.

Before you begin

Keep these ready

The platform will guide you through each step and indicate what is required.

Producer details

  • PAN and GST, where applicable
  • Bank details and cancelled cheque
  • KFCC Producer Registration Number
  • Registered address and address proof

Organisation documents

  • Partnership Agreement, where applicable
  • Authorisation Letter, where applicable
  • Board Resolution for Private Limited Companies
  • Organisation type details

Film & technical assets

  • Final film master, minimum 2K
  • Poster, banner, trailer and subtitles
  • Synopsis, cast and crew details
  • Rights Holder Affidavit and Producer Authorisation Letter, where applicable
